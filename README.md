# FORMIQ — Cinematic Product Experience

> **“Every response has a story.”**

An award-level, continuous cinematic product experience for **FORMIQ**.

Engineered as an interactive product film and spatial data installation rather than a conventional website or dashboard. Built with a bespoke Three.js WebGL particle engine, cinematic camera choreography, negative-space editorial typography, multi-variable intelligence lenses, and tactile spatial feedback.

## 🎬 The 12 Continuous Scenes

1. **SCENE 01 — THE VOID**
   - Pure black screen with an expanding singularity point of light.
   - Letter-by-letter reveal: *“WHAT HAPPENS AFTER YOU ASK?”*
   - Pause. *“YOU GET RESPONSES.”*
   - Thousands of particles burst into fluid motion as **FORMIQ** (*“Every response has a story.”*) slowly emerges.

2. **SCENE 02 — THE CHAOS OF RESPONSES**
   - The logo recedes as thousands of response fragments fly in 3D toward the viewer with depth, blur, and parallax.
   - Monumental statement: *“WE COLLECT. WE STORE. WE FORGET TO UNDERSTAND.”*
   - The chaotic swarm freezes in 3D space.

3. **SCENE 03 — FORM**
   - Chaos collapses into a single elegant digital form tablet floating in 3D space.
   - Statement: *“DESCRIBE WHAT YOU WANT TO KNOW.”*
   - Live natural-language prompt typing: *“Create a student feedback form about laboratory facilities.”*
   - The system synthesizes and generates structured questions in real time.

4. **SCENE 04 — COLLECT**
   - The form multiplies into thousands of streaming instances.
   - Ingestion counter accelerates dynamically: `1` → `10` → `50` → `100` → `250` → `500 RESPONSES`.
   - Complete silence as the screen fills with streaming data.

5. **SCENE 05 — INTELLIGENCE AWAKENS (Signature Scene)**
   - 500 response particles automatically self-organize into 5 distinct spatial clusters:
     `INFRASTRUCTURE`, `SCHEDULING`, `FACULTY`, `RESOURCES`, `EXPERIENCE`.
   - Interconnecting neural vector lines form between dependent concepts.
   - *“PATTERNS ARE HIDDEN IN THE NOISE. FORMIQ FINDS THEM.”*

6. **SCENE 06 — INSIGHT**
   - Screen clears into vast negative space.
   - Center reveal: *“Laboratory infrastructure is the most recurring concern, with dissatisfaction concentrated among first-year students.”*
   - 4 Interactive Lenses revealing the same dataset: `TREND`, `SENTIMENT`, `GROUP DIFFERENCE`, `RECURRING ISSUE`.

7. **SCENE 07 — THE MOMENT OF DECISION**
   - Emotional turning point: *“AN INSIGHT IS NOT THE END. WHAT SHOULD WE DO?”*
   - Generates an actionable implementation plan:
     - **Priority 01:** Improve laboratory availability (48 Hours)
     - **Priority 02:** Optimize lab scheduling (Week 2)
     - **Priority 03:** Increase system availability during peak sessions (Ongoing)

8. **SCENE 08 — THE PIPELINE**
   - Dramatic 3D zoom out revealing the continuous spatial pipeline:
     `FORM → RESPONSES → ANALYSIS → PATTERNS → INSIGHTS → RECOMMENDATIONS → ACTION`.
   - Drag/orbit the 3D data-flow with mouse inertia; hover each stage to inspect systemic telemetry.

9. **SCENE 09 — MULTIPLE WORLDS**
   - The central FormIQ core stays in center surrounded by 4 sector portals:
     `EDUCATION`, `HR & PEOPLE`, `CUSTOMER RESEARCH`, `EVENTS & SUMMITS`.
   - Hovering over each sector morphs the telemetry and surrounding particle flow.
   - *“ONE QUESTION ENGINE. ENDLESS DECISIONS.”*

10. **SCENE 10 — TECHNOLOGY**
    - Exploded 3D system architecture with floating, breathing vertical planes:
      `INTERFACE → FORM ENGINE → DATA LAYER → AI ANALYTICS → INSIGHT ENGINE → ACTION ENGINE`.
    - Stack rotates dynamically; hover layers to inspect technical decoupling.

11. **SCENE 11 — THE TRANSFORMATION**
    - Particles return to a harmonious, crystalline state.
    - Continuous morphing cycle:
      `RAW RESPONSES → STRUCTURED DATA → UNDERSTANDING → DECISION → ACTION`.

12. **SCENE 12 — FINAL**
    - Screen fades to pure black.
    - Line 1: *“FORMS SHOULD NOT END WITH A SPREADSHEET.”*
    - Dramatic pause.
    - Line 2: *“THEY SHOULD END WITH A DECISION.”*
    - Very slowly: **FORMIQ** — *“Every response has a story.”*
